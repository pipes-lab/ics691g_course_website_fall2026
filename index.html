How grades work

Component Weight Details
HW0 (Onboarding & Concept Check) 5% Required to ensure development environment, access to Koa (UH HPC), and foundational concepts are solid.
Midterm Exams (3 Total) 45% 15% each. Regularly assesses understanding of algorithmic and theoretical concepts.
Final Exam (Cumulative) 25% Comprehensive, heavily weighted toward advanced topics covered in final weeks (Weeks 13-15).
Individual Course Project 25% Proposal (2.5%), Mid-term Checkpoint (2.5%), Final Report & Code (10%), Final Presentation (10%).

Policies

Late Policy & Make-Ups

HW0 late submissions lose 10% per day, with a maximum of 3 late days total. Project components must be submitted on time. No make-up midterms will be offered except in the case of extreme, prolonged, documented emergencies.

Lecture Recordings & Attendance

All lectures are recorded. Attendance is taken to determine recording access. Immediate access to recordings is only available to students present for that specific session. If you are absent, access is delayed until the day before the relevant midterm, or at the start of the reading period for the final. Exceptions granting immediate access are made for excusable absences (e.g., documented illness, university travel, severe emergencies, religious observances) via an email request.

Homework Assignments (HW1-HW5)

HW1 through HW5 are completely optional and will not be graded. However, they are highly recommended as they serve as primary study guides for mid-terms and the final exam.

Academic Honesty

All work submitted must be your own. Discussing concepts with classmates is encouraged, but final work (code, written answers, reports, exams) must be done individually. Any external sources must be properly cited. Plagiarism or cheating is handled according to the University of Hawai'i Student Conduct Code.

Disability Access

Students needing accommodations are encouraged to contact the KOKUA Program (Student Services Center, Room 013, 808-956-7511, kokua@hawaii.edu) as soon as possible for timely implementation.

Communication

Check the course website regularly for announcements. Post questions in the 'Discussions' tab. Allow up to 24-48 hours for email responses, especially on weekends.
